This page highlights Python-based projects completed by Veronica Estrada during her time at the University of Colorado Boulder. These projects span topics such as planetary system modeling, data processing, and analysis of observational datasets.

Data sources include instruments like SDO, GOES, MAVEN, and various ground-based telescopes. Due to access restrictions, raw data is not included here, but many datasets can be found publicly on government platforms like NASA or NOAA.
